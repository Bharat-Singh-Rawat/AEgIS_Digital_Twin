Beam

          
          
Mode

          

            
Custom Geant4 11.4 application (hbar_g4/): one e⁺ per event from the positron line through the real fields into the silica, Geant4 stopping → positronium (7 %, 142 ns o-Ps decay to 3γ) → 205+1693 nm laser → Rydberg Ps (motional-Stark limit in the local B) → charge exchange in the p̄ cloud taken from a G4beamline run → H̄ (falls under gravity) → gratings and detector, where it annihilates into a p̄ + e⁺ that Geant4 annihilates (π, γ). Rare steps use weights; results are per shot of 2×10⁶ e⁺.

            

              
              
              
              
              
              
            

          

          

            
AEgIS 2023 ballistic sequence (Volponi 2024, §4.2.4): P12 → A1 parabola, P11 wall, A0 reflector, Ps target in, degrader out. The p̄ cloud is launched from P12; G4beamline records every p̄ at the formation time.

Positrons
From the ²²Na source to the bunch: Geant4 through the capsule and the solid-neon moderator, then the slow beam, the buffer-gas trap and the accumulator.

      

        

          
1 · Source + moderator (G4beamline)

          
²²Na (β⁺ 90.3 %, 546.44 keV endpoint) on a Ta backing behind a 5 µm Ti window (iThemba capsule), solid neon on a Cu cone at 7 K, 120 G guide field. Geant4 tracks every β⁺ until it stops; re-emission from the neon is a diffusion-escape model (Geant4 has no moderation physics).

3 · Full positron line in Geant4

          
Custom Geant4 11.4 app (hbar_g4/positron_line.cc): the same source and moderator, then re-emission from the neon surface, guide fields 120 → 250 → 750 G, moderator bias and slow-e⁺ filter, and the 3-stage N₂ buffer-gas trap with an e⁺–N₂ process (a¹Π excitation, Ps formation; Marler & Surko 2005). Every positron is tracked from the source until it is trapped, lost or escapes.
